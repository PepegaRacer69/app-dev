# app-dev
  Some information about my favorite racing game series or franchise **Need For Speed**
  - The Series started in 1994 as a simple but detailed 3D racing game for the time.
  - From 1994 to 2002, the series solely focused on racing exotic cars on exotic locations with emphasis on police chases.
  - In 2003, the series departed from it's Exotic car roots in favor for a more urban style street racing that emphasizes on vehicle customization, giving you the feel of actually owning the car you picked, and these cars are not exotics, they're your typical city cars that you might often see in your neighborhoods thanks to the popularity of the first **Fast & Furious** film from 2001.
  - This formula worked well for the series as the first game with this style, **Need For Speed Underground** sold 15 million copies worldwide.
  - In 2004, the series got their first open-world racing game, **Need For Speed Underground 2** and to this day, it's still fondly remembered by a lot of people.
  - In 2005, cops made a return in the series along with exotic cars in a more gritty and dirtier setting, that being **Need For Speed Most Wanted**, and this was the best decision that happened to the franchise, as this game sold over 18 million copies worldwide as of now, also making it the best-selling Need For Speed game to date.
  - From 2007 to 2013, the franchise entered an identity crisis, with 3 track racer/circuit focused games being released, those being **Need For Speed ProStreet**, and **Need For Speed Shift 1 & 2: Unleashed**.
  - In 2014, the series got it's first ever movie adaptation, starring **Aaron Paul**, who's famously known as **Jesse Pinkman from the tv series Breaking Bad**, this is my favorite movie to date and is super underrated, even though I watch the Fast & Furious movies religiously.
  - As of today, there is no news for a new Need For Speed game being developed since the franchise was shelved by EA recently.
